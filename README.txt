PRISM STUDIO — SITE BUNDLE 1

Files included: homepage, Privacy Policy, Terms & Conditions, stylesheet, site interactions, and the Prism Studio SVG logo.

UPLOAD TO GITHUB
1. Download and unzip this bundle.
2. Open your GitHub repository.
3. Choose Add file → Upload files.
4. Upload the contents of the prism-studio folder (index.html, privacy.html, terms.html, README.txt, and assets/).
5. Commit the uploaded files.

The website is static. Project enquiries open your Instagram page. Email and phone are listed for general contact. The Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions are drafts and should be reviewed and completed before relying on them.
