04 / FAQ

A few useful

What does a typical project look like?

Every project is shaped around its goals, team and timeline. Share a little about what you have in mind and we can outline a suitable process.

Can you join a project already in progress?

Yes. Prism can support a specific stage, from editorial and motion through to 3D production and finishing.

What do you need to get started?

A short brief, a sense of the intended audience and any existing material are a helpful start. We can work through the rest together.
